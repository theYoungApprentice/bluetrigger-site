Privacy Policy — Blue Trigger

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Blue Trigger ("the app") is a local-first Android utility that runs an action when one of your paired Bluetooth devices connects. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

Blue Trigger does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads, no accounts, and no network communication by the app itself.

Data the app stores on your device

  • Your device rules. For each device you configure, the app stores its Bluetooth address and display name, the chosen action, and the action argument (an app package name or a stream URL). Saved stream presets (name + link) are stored too. All of this is kept locally via Android DataStore and never leaves the device.

Bluetooth

The app reads your list of bonded (paired) Bluetooth devices so you can pick one, and observes connection events to run the action you chose. A device address is only used as a local selector; the app acts only on devices that are already bonded. Device identifiers are not logged or transmitted.

Installed apps

For the "open app" action, the app queries the list of launchable apps to populate the in-app picker. This list is not stored or transmitted.

Opening apps and streams

The "open app" and "open stream" actions post a local notification that, when activated, opens the chosen app or hands the stream URL to your own media player via a standard Android intent. The app does not fetch or proxy stream content itself.

Permissions

  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN (and legacy BLUETOOTH/BLUETOOTH_ADMIN on Android 11 and below) — to read bonded devices and observe connections. BLUETOOTH_SCAN is declared with neverForLocation; the app does not request location.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the service and action notifications on Android 13+.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE — to run the detection service with a visible notification.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — so the "open app"/"open stream" action can surface automatically where allowed.
  • VIBRATE — for the vibrate and ring actions.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to resume detection after a reboot if you enabled it.

Data sharing

None. No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.
